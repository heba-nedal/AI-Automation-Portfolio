# Tiny Wins — Mini Brand Brief

## 1. Project Overview

**Tiny Wins** is an AI-powered personal growth platform that helps individuals record their daily achievements, reflect on their progress, and appreciate the small steps that contribute to personal growth.

## 2. Target Audience

The primary audience includes individuals who:

- Are interested in personal development and self-improvement.
- Want to build positive daily habits.
- Want to keep track of their achievements.
- Need a simple way to reflect on their progress over time.

## 3. Content Goal

The content aims to:

- Introduce Tiny Wins and its main purpose.
- Encourage people to recognize the value of small achievements.
- Show how recording daily wins can help users understand their progress.
- Encourage users to explore and try the platform.

## 4. Tone of Voice

The communication style should be:

- **Encouraging:** Motivate users without creating pressure.
- **Warm:** Make users feel comfortable and supported.
- **Positive:** Focus on progress and possibilities.
- **Simple:** Use clear and easy-to-understand language.
- **Human:** Communicate naturally rather than sounding overly technical.

## 5. Visual Direction

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex Code | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Cream | `#F8F5EF` | Main background |
| Forest Green | `#263D34` | Primary text and visual identity |
| Terracotta | `#D96F48` | Highlights and accents |
| Sage Green | `#D9E2D5` | Supporting backgrounds and growth elements |

### Visual Style

- Clean and minimal layouts.
- Nature-inspired elements such as plants, leaves, and growth.
- Soft colors and balanced spacing.
- Friendly illustrations and simple visual storytelling.
- Consistent use of the Tiny Wins visual identity.

## 6. Key Message

**Every Small Win Matters.**

Tiny Wins encourages people to celebrate small achievements and discover how far they have come.

## 7. Call to Action

**Start recording your small wins and celebrate your growth, one step at a time.**
